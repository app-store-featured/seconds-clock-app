The iPhone never shows seconds. The Lock Screen, the status bar and Apple's own clock widgets
all stop at the minute. Seconds Clock fills that gap: a live seconds display that ticks every
second, wherever you want to see it.

## What it does

### Widgets

Put seconds on the Lock Screen, below the clock or in the small slot above it.
On the Home Screen, pick a small, medium or large widget (extra-large on iPad) and style it as
transparent, Liquid Glass, a solid color or a gradient.

### Floating clock

Keep the time, down to the millisecond if you like, in the Dynamic Island
while you use other apps.

### Nightstand clock

A quiet full-screen clock with the date, time and seconds. Use it on a
stand, a desk or in StandBy, upright or on its side.

### Make it yours

Built-in fonts or any font on your device, preset or custom colors, light or
dark appearance, an optional time zone label and tenths of a second. Seconds Clock follows your
text size and 12/24-hour setting, and it comes in 13 languages.

### iPhone Duo and Shortcuts

Works on iPhone Duo in both orientations. Its actions are available in Shortcuts and Siri.

## FAQ

**Can the iPhone Lock Screen show seconds?**

Not by itself. Seconds Clock adds a Lock Screen widget that does.

**How do I add the widget to my Home Screen?**

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap **Edit**, then **Add Widget**, and search for *Live Seconds*.

**Does the floating clock drain the battery?**

No. Nothing runs in the background.

**What does it run on?**

iPhone, iPhone Duo and iPad with iOS 18 or later, including iOS 27.
